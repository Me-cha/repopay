### 06.04 — Reconcile item outcomes and preserve award entitlement

**Files:** `packages/server/src/paypal/status-map.ts`, `packages/server/src/settlement/payout.ts`, `packages/server/src/actions/status.ts`, `packages/server/test/integration/payout-status.test.ts`.

**Depends on:** 06.03 and phase prerequisites.

**Interface and implementation contract:** reconcilePayout(decisionId:string): Promise<void>. Read authoritative batch/item state; PAID requires every awarded item currently successfully delivered. Unknown/held/unclaimed/failed/returned items retain reason and award. Distinguish successful paid principal from the unpaid remainder; transfer attempts do not double-count an award. A verified later return/reversal after PAID moves the affected value from paid to unpaid award and marks PAYING + attention without resending automatically. Preserve the original receipt/history and show the new delivery problem; the canonical state machine includes this PAID → PAYING transition.

**Review completion contract:** Fence concurrent provider reads with observation versions. Periodically revisit PAID items in rotating bounded pages so missed return webhooks are discovered; terminal success stops rapid polling, not reconciliation. Test slow stale SUCCESS racing RETURNED and a missed return on a previously PAID bounty.
